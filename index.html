Progression & Overview

From Foundation to Intermediate Classical Studies

Having completed the foundational level, the student now advances to engage directly with intermediate classical texts according to the Manhaj of the Salaf (Ahlul Hadith). Level 2 deepens your grasp of Fiqh through detailed rulings, strengthens your 'Aqīdah through works that address doubts, and immerses you in the Prophetic tradition through the renowned forty hadith of Imām An-Nawawī. This is where knowledge transforms into conviction.

Deepened Fiqh Detailed Asanīd Methodical Aqeedah
Level 2 Curriculum

The Classical Texts You Will Study

Six core disciplines drawn from the finest works of Salafi scholarship.

تفسير
Tafsīr
Mukhtaṣar Tafsīr Al-Baghawī — Imām Al-Baghawī raḥimahullāh
Covering Sūrah Al-Fātiḥah, Sūrah Aḍ-Ḍuḥā to An-Nās
A concise commentary on the Quran by Imām Al-Baghawī, known for its clarity and reliance upon narrations of the Salaf. Students will study the exegesis of Al-Fātiḥah and the short surahs from Aḍ-Ḍuḥā to An-Nās.
Click to expand
عقیدہ
'Aqīdah
Kashf Ash-Shubuhāt, Al-Uṣūl As-Sittah & Faḍl Al-Islām — Shaykh Muḥammad ibn 'Abd al-Wahhāb raḥimahullāh
Three foundational treatises by Shaykh Al-Islām Muḥammad ibn 'Abd al-Wahhāb. Kashf Ash-Shubuhāt dismantles doubts around Tawḥīd, Al-Uṣūl As-Sittah establishes six core principles, and Faḍl Al-Islām highlights the virtue and superiority of Islām.
Click to expand
ادب
Adab
Tadhkirat As-Sāmi' wa Al-Mutakallim — Imām Ibn Jamā'ah raḥimahullāh
A classical manual on the etiquettes of the student and teacher of knowledge by Imām Ibn Jamā'ah. It covers the manners expected during learning, teaching, and scholarly exchange — essential for every serious student.
Click to expand
حدیث
Ḥadīth
Al-Arba'īn An-Nawawiyyah — Imām Yaḥyā ibn Sharaf An-Nawawī raḥimahullāh
Kitāb Al-'Ilm from Mishkāt Al-Maṣābīḥ — Imām Al-Khaṭīb At-Tabrīzī raḥimahullāh
The celebrated forty hadith collection of Imām An-Nawawī — each hadith a foundational pillar of the religion. Complemented by Kitāb Al-'Ilm from Mishkāt Al-Maṣābīḥ, covering the virtues, etiquettes, and obligations of seeking sacred knowledge.
Click to expand
فقہ
Fiqh
Shurūṭ Aṣ-Ṣalāh wa Arkānuhā wa Wājibātuhā & Ṣifah Ṣalāt An-Nabī ﷺ — Shaykh 'Abd al-'Azīz ibn Bāz raḥimahullāh
Two essential works on the jurisprudence of prayer by Shaykh Ibn Bāz. The first covers the conditions, pillars, and obligations of the prayer in detail. The second describes the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ from beginning to end based on authentic narrations.
Click to expand
سیرت
Sīrah
Mukhtaṣar Fī As-Sīrah — Al-Ḥāfiẓ 'Abd Al-Ghanī Al-Maqdisī raḥimahullāh
A concise yet comprehensive biography of the Prophet ﷺ by Al-Ḥāfiẓ 'Abd Al-Ghanī Al-Maqdisī. It covers the key events of the Prophetic life in a structured, scholarly format ideal for intermediate students.
