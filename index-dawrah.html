Dawrah Curriculum

The 7 Core Disciplines & Treatises

Anchored upon the Quran, the authentic Sunnah, and classical treatises of the Salaf as-Salih.

01
Ḥadīth & Tazkiyah

The Importance of Ikhlāṣ: Ḥadīth “Innamā al-A‘māl bin-Niyyāt”

أهمية الإخلاص: حديث «إنما الأعمال بالنيات»
Shaykh Zafar Alam bin Ziya ur-Rahman

The bedrock of all actions in Islam. Exegesis of the prophetic maxim establishing that outer deeds hold no weight without sincere intention for Allah alone.

02
‘Aqīdah & Tawḥīd

Kashf ash-Shubuhāt

كشف الشبهات للإمام المجدد محمد بن عبد الوهاب
Ustadh Muhammad Abu Bakr al-Lahori

A foundational manual dismantling common philosophical and polytheistic doubts with decisive, unassailable proofs straight from the Holy Qur'an.

03
Fiqh aṣ-Ṣalāh

Shurūṭ aṣ-Ṣalāh wa Arkānuhā wa Wājibātuhā

شروط الصلاة وأركانها وواجباتها
Shaykh Muhammad Aakif al-Makki

A structured and systematic jurisprudence breakdown of the nine conditions, fourteen pillars, and eight obligations of the daily Islamic prayer.

04
Fiqh aṭ-Ṭahārah (Sisters)

Bāb al-Ḥayḍ from ad-Durar al-Bahiyyah

باب الحيض من الدرر البهية للشوكاني
Ustadhah Bint Raees Ahmad al-Athariyyah

Specialized fiqh of female purification, answering practical contemporary scenarios regarding menstruation, purification, and worship with scholarly precision.

05
Musalsalāt & Sanad

Al-Ḥadīth al-Musalsal bil-Awwaliyyah

الحديث المسلسل بالأولية «الراحمون يرحمهم الرحمن»
Abu Mu‘awiyah Muhammad Qurayshi

The historic first narration imparted from teacher to student throughout the centuries, establishing the divine virtue of showing mercy to the creation.

06
Manhaj & Arabic Ode

Al-Qaṣīdah al-Waḍḍāḥiyyah

القصيدة الوضاحية في الدفاع عن أم المؤمنين عائشة
Abu Mu‘awiyah Muhammad Qurayshi

The celebrated didactic poem spoken from the tongue of the Mother of the Believers, affirming her honor, chastity, and unparalleled virtues from the Qur'an.

07
Tafsīr al-Qur’ān

Tafsīr of Sūrah al-Fātiḥah and Sūrah al-Kahf

تفسير سورة الفاتحة وسورة الكهف
Shaykh Hassan Sarim

Unfolding the spiritual depths of the opening Surah repeated in every rak'ah, along with the weekly fortress of Surah al-Kahf that illuminates between two Fridays.
